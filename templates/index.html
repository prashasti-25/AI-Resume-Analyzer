What it does

  • Extracts contact info, skills, education, and experience from your resume PDF

How it works

  • Your resume is parsed into structured text and skills
  • Both texts are cleaned and vectorized with TF-IDF
  • Cosine similarity and skill overlap combine into one score
  • Every past analysis is saved to your history
